# Learning path

Ordered by when the roadmap needs each concept. Resources are primary where possible. Links marked (P1) were opened by the Phase 1 thread; (today) were opened by me on 2026-11-06. Books without a link are named but were not opened, so treat them as Unverified recommendations.

| # | Concept | Needed by | Resources | Self-check exercise |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Simple vs log returns, compounding, annualisation | D-2, D-3 | pandas time-series guide (today): https://pandas.pydata.org/docs/user_guide/timeseries.html ; [Lo 2002](https://ideas.repec.org/a/taf/ufajxx/v58y2002i4p36-52.html) (today) on annualising Sharpe | By hand: daily returns +1%, −1%, +2% → compound return; explain why mean of log returns ≠ log of mean return |
| 2 | pandas alignment, shifting, month-end resampling | D-2…D-5 | pandas time-series guide (above) | Write `mom_12_1` for one asset with `shift` and verify against a hand-computed 15-month example |
| 3 | Reproducible Python: uv, lockfiles, pydantic configs, CI | S-1, S-2 | [GitHub Actions billing](https://docs.github.com/en/billing/concepts/product-billing/github-actions) (today) for free CI; uv and pydantic official docs (not opened, Unverified) | Fresh clone → `uv sync --frozen` → tests green, on Windows and in CI |
| 4 | Testing: unit, property-based, fixtures, coverage | S-2, E-*, V-* | [Hypothesis docs](https://hypothesis.readthedocs.io/en/latest/) (today); pytest docs (not opened) | Write a hypothesis test that fails on a deliberately bugged turnover function |

| 6 | Momentum: cross-sectional, industry, time-series | D-4, R-1 | [Jegadeesh & Titman 1993](https://ideas.repec.org/a/bla/jfinan/v48y1993i1p65-91.html) (P1); [Moskowitz & Grinblatt 1999](https://ideas.repec.org/a/bla/jfinan/v54y1999i4p1249-1290.html) (P1, today); [Moskowitz, Ooi & Pedersen 2012](https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=2089463) (P1) | Write 12-1 industry momentum as maths; say why the last month is skipped |
| 7 | Volatility scaling and momentum crashes | R-2 | [Barroso & Santa-Clara 2015](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:eee:jfinec:v:116:y:2015:i:1:p:111-120) (today); [Daniel & Moskowitz 2016](https://www.nber.org/papers/w20439) (P1) | Explain why losers have high beta after a bear market and what happens in a rebound |
| 8 | Transaction costs, turnover, capacity | E-4, T-3 | [Novy-Marx & Velikov](https://nber.org/papers/w20721) (P1); [Frazzini, Israel & Moskowitz](https://www.aqr.com/Insights/Research/Working-Paper/Trading-Costs-of-Asset-Pricing-Anomalies) (today) | Given monthly one-way turnover 60% and 10 bp cost, compute annual cost drag |

| 10 | Factor regressions | R-4 | [Ken French Data Library](https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/data_library.html) (today) | Regress B3 on FF3 + Mom; interpret alpha and Mom beta |
| 11 | Time-series CV: walk-forward, purging, embargo, CPCV | M-2, M-5 | [López de Prado 2018, ch. 7, 12](https://www.wiley-vch.de/de/fachgebiete/finanzen-wirtschaft-recht/advances-in-financial-machine-learning-978-1-119-48208-6) (P1, table of contents); [purgedcv](https://pypi.org/project/purgedcv/) (P1) | Explain why a 1-month label plus a 1-day fill lag needs a purge of 1 month + 1 day |
| 12 | Gradient boosting and ranking metrics | M-3 | [LightGBM parameters](https://lightgbm.readthedocs.io/en/stable/Parameters.html) (P1); [Gu, Kelly & Xiu 2020](https://nber.org/papers/w25398) (P1) | Explain what `min_data_in_leaf` does to overfitting with 49 assets per month |
| 13 | Multiple testing: DSR, PBO, haircut Sharpe | B-3 | [Bailey & López de Prado 2014](https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=2460551) (P1); [Bailey et al. 2015](https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=2326253) (P1); [Harvey & Liu, Backtesting](https://people.duke.edu/~charvey/Research/Published_Papers/P120_Backtesting.PDF) (today); [Harvey, Liu & Zhu](https://www.nber.org/papers/w20592) (P1) | Compute PSR for a toy series and match purgedcv; explain why Harvey & Liu reject the 50% haircut rule |
| 14 | Feature importance: permutation, grouping, SHAP | F-1…F-5 | [scikit-learn permutation importance](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/permutation_importance.html) (P1); [multicollinear example](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/auto_examples/inspection/plot_permutation_importance_multicollinear.html) (today); [SHAP TreeExplainer](https://shap.readthedocs.io/en/stable/generated/shap.TreeExplainer.html) (today); [Lundberg & Lee 2017](https://papers.nips.cc/paper/7062-a) (P1) | On a toy dataset with two copies of one feature, show single-feature permutation importance near zero and group importance high |
| 15 | Complexity critique | M-4 | [Nagel 2025](https://www.nber.org/papers/w34104) (P1); [Kelly, Malamud & Zhou 2024](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:bla:jfinan:v:79:y:2024:i:1:p:459-503) (P1) | In two sentences each, state both sides and the test you run |
| 16 | Model risk and documentation | W-1 | [SR 26-2](https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/srletters/sr2602.htm) (P1) | Write the model card's "monitoring triggers" section from memory |
